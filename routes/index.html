TELEMETRY
  • 🔬
    External Stopwatch (Avidemux): Precision verification is conducted frame-by-frame using Avidemux.exe software in millisecond format 00:00.000.
  • ⚠️
    Timebug Threshold: A Timebug is officially declared when the discrepancy between the in-game timer and the frame count exceeds 2 FPS (6 hundredths of a second).
  • ⚖️
    Tie Resolution: For times with differences under 10 hundredths (0.10s), stewards will calculate the real time by frame count and publish the corrected time.
  • 🛡️
    Anti-Cheat Policy: Use of Speedhacks, trainers, slowdown scripts, or steering macros results in a permanent server ban and nullification of all historical records.
